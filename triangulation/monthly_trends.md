# Monthly Trends — Review + XHS + YouTube + Instagram + Facebook Activity Over Time

Full available range: 2014-08 to 2026-07 (144 months), per brand, zero/blank-filled for months with no activity. LIHKG is excluded — it only carries a relative "N months ago" age string, no absolute post date, so it can't be placed on a monthly timeline. YouTube and Instagram comments, and Facebook reviews, ARE included (real absolute timestamps) — YouTube/Instagram filtered to on-topic comments on brand-relevant content only, see youtube_scraper.py/youtube_signals.py and instagram_scraper.py/instagram_signals.py; Facebook reviews carry a rule-derived sentiment (from the reviewer's own recommend/not-recommend flag, not LLM-labeled like the other sources) — see facebook_reviews_merge.py/facebook_signals.py.

**This CSV (`monthly_trends.csv`) is the reusable artifact** — a full month x brand grid intended for reuse in future event-study/lead-lag scripts, not just the charts below.
